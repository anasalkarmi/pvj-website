KEY POINTS
- The RCPCH strategy was shaped by members' answers to 70 questions asked in 2024 and has four goals.
- The first three goals are to support members, advocate for children and young people, and use research and quality improvement.
- The fourth goal is to increase international impact, including supporting members abroad and humanitarian global work.
