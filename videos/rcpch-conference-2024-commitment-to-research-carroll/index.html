Key points

  • The annual conference is a great opportunity for paediatricians to share their research, whatever speciality they work in.
  • A huge number of abstracts were submitted, which shows the commitment of paediatricians to research across the United Kingdom.

Professor Will Carroll, Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health joint officer for research, introduces the annual conference and highlights the commitment shown by paediatricians to research across the United Kingdom. A specific focus of the conference was on health inequalities which ties with the RCPCH mission and objectives.
