RCPCH Conference 2024: Commitment to Research

  • RCPCH Conference 2024
  • Birmingham
  • Published 26 January 2025
  • 0:29 min
  • WC
    Professor Will CarrollRoyal College of Paediatrics and Child Health joint officer for research

Professor Will Carroll, Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health joint officer for research, introduces the annual conference and highlights the commitment shown by paediatricians to research across the United Kingdom. A specific focus of the conference was on health inequalities which ties with the RCPCH mission and objectives.

This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2024 in Birmingham, UK. These works are owned by the Paediatric Video Journal (PVJ) and are protected by copyright laws and treaties around the world. All rights are reserved.
