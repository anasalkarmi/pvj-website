Key points

  • The work is tied to the college's mission objectives on health inequalities, and to health and well-being research.

Neil Meemaduma, Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, discusses the aim of this years conference; 'working better together'. As associate director for research and quality improvement, he describes the opportunity provided for members to present research, audits and quality improvement studies. A specific focus of the conference was on health inequalities which ties with the RCPCH mission and objectives.
