PICUlearn: building a paediatric intensive care learning community

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 1:52 min
  • Dr Ikechukwu OkongwuPaediatric Registrar at Addenbrooke's Hospital and PICU GRID trainee

Dr Ikechukwu Okongwu, Paediatric Registrar at Addenbrooke's Hospital and PICU GRID trainee, provides insights into PICUlearn, an online learning community for paediatric intensive care. He explains how the project grew from the need to bring scattered educational resources together in one place. The interview introduces its approach to sharing teaching, videos, research updates, guidance, webinars and events, with particular support for trainees and those beginning to learn about PICU. Dr Okongwu discusses the value of participation and feedback from across the multidisciplinary team, and his ambition to build a shared space for learning and professional development.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
