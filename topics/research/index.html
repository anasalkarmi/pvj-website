Topic

Research and clinical trials

How paediatric evidence is built: trials, surveillance, research networks and capacity.

The videos cover randomised trials and adaptive platform trials such as PIVOTAL and GASTRIC-PICU, along with precision medicine using biomarkers. They also describe PICANet data, surveillance of rare childhood diseases, patient and public involvement, and building research capacity through a Trainee Research Network and Assistant Research Practitioners.
