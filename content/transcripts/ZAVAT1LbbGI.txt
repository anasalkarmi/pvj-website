KEY POINTS
- A two-cycle retrospective audit at Great North Children's Hospital looked at escalation of, and response to, deterioration before unplanned PICU admission.
- Results improved across the two cycles, possibly helped by sharing findings, teaching on documentation and a paediatric deterioration nurse specialist.
- The data helped secure funding for a paediatric critical care outreach team, and a third cycle will look at its impact.
