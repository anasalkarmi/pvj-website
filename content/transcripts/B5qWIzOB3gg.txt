KEY POINTS
- The role of the AHP representative on PCCS Council is to promote all AHP professions, not only physiotherapy, within the paediatric critical care community.
- AHPs across the UK are encouraged to attend events, learn, disseminate what they learn, improve practice and share their work.
- AHPs are encouraged to consider joining PCCS to support collaboration across the UK.
