KEY POINTS
- PICUlearn came out of a need to bring scattered paediatric intensive care teaching and videos together in one interactive place.
- It offers a weekly newsletter covering research, guidelines, videos, teaching, webinars, conferences and events.
- It is aimed especially at trainees starting out, with courses on the basics of paediatric intensive care, and the aim of adding official courses with CPD certificates.
