Neurology

The developing brain: seizures, brain injury, neuromuscular and neurodevelopmental conditions.

Topics include intracranial pressure monitoring in traumatic brain injury, delays in treating status epilepticus, neonatal brain MRI and spinal muscular atrophy. The videos also describe sleep-disordered breathing in neuromuscular disorders, virtual reality simulation for autism spectrum disorder, ADHD and the development of cognitive empathy in children with neurological disorders.
