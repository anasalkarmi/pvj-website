KEY POINTS
- Adult ARDS evidence, including proning and ECMO trials, does not simply apply to children, whose compliance, airways, FRC and lungs differ.
- Paediatric trials such as the PROSPECT trial have been negative, and there is nothing like the evidence base available in adults.
- Next steps are probably precision medicine, enriching trial populations and tailoring treatments to the phenotypes or endotypes being managed.
