Supporting Patient Safety in Paediatrics: RCPCH Resources and Initiatives

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 27 August 2025
  • 1:30 min
  • JB
    Jonathan BamberHead of Quality Improvement at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health

Jonathan Bamber, Head of Quality Improvement at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, provides insights into the ongoing efforts to support patient safety for children, young people, and RCPCH members. The discussion highlights key initiatives such as the SAFE programme and the development of web pages and resources dedicated to patient safety. Mr Bamber introduces the RCPCH patient safety portal, which offers a wealth of information including podcasts on safety culture, psychological safety, learning from harm, and addressing inequality in safety. He also details the launch of a safety e-bulletin, distributed to members every two months, summarizing the latest developments in paediatric patient safety. Mr Bamber encourages all members to engage with these resources and invites non-members to access previous editions via the RCPCH website. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
