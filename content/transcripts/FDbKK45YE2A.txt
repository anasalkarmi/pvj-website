KEY POINTS
- Families visiting the hospital were asked why they had not chosen to vaccinate their children.
- Between 10 and 20% of families had chosen not to have their children vaccinated, and the majority of reasons were probably not supported by scientific facts.
- Concerns were often about something the family had been told or an internet concern, and many of the sites that informed them contained misinformation.
