Transition to adult care: The Inbetweeners

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 4:09 min
  • KM
    Dr Katherine (Katie) MalbonConsultant Paediatrician and Lead Clinical Co-ordinator for Child Health at NCEPOD

Dr Katherine (Katie) Malbon, Consultant Paediatrician and Lead Clinical Co-ordinator for Child Health at NCEPOD, provides insights into The Inbetweeners study and the transition from paediatric to adult services for young people with complex conditions. She discusses gaps in the organisation and delivery of transition care, including staff training, involvement of young people and coordination between specialties. The interview explores recommendations for making developmentally appropriate healthcare part of everyday practice, strengthening multidisciplinary working and ensuring transition has organisational support. Dr Malbon also reflects on the progress reported by services since the study and the importance of keeping young people's needs at the centre of change.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
