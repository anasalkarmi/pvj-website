Emergency care

Recognising and treating the acutely unwell child, from the emergency department to the ward.

The videos look at recognising deterioration, delays in treating status epilepticus, and the national paediatric early warning systems with the SPOT pilot. Others describe point-of-care testing for Group A Streptococcus, oral fluid challenges, a skin conditions guideline and accidental adrenaline auto-injector injuries in children.
