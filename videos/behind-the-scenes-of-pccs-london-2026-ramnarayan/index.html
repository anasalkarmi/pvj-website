Behind the scenes of PCCS London 2026

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 2:29 min
  • PR
    Professor Padmanabhan RamnarayanProfessor of Paediatric Critical Care at Imperial College London

Professor Padmanabhan Ramnarayan, Professor of Paediatric Critical Care at Imperial College London, reflects on organising PCCS London 2026 at Lord’s. He discusses choosing the venue, developing the scientific programme and bringing together a broad range of expertise. The interview explores the practical work behind a conference, from logistics to collaboration with event organisers and the contribution of volunteers. Professor Ramnarayan highlights the teamwork involved in creating a meeting for the paediatric critical-care community and the value of providing opportunities for colleagues to learn, exchange ideas and connect.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
