Work with PVJ

PVJ works with societies and conference organisers who would like short, professional video interviews recorded at their event and shared with a wider audience.

What PVJ does at an event

We record short interviews on site with presenters, delegates and, where appropriate, parents and young people who take part in research. We edit the interviews and publish them in the PVJ library, each with the speaker's name and role and a written summary. Videos are hosted on the PVJ YouTube channel and shared on the PVJ LinkedIn page. They are free to watch.

Example: PCCS Conference 2026

For the Paediatric Critical Care Society (PCCS) Conference 2026 at Lord's in London, PVJ worked in partnership with PCCS. We recorded 48 interviews and produced:

  • an individual interview video for each speaker, with on-screen name captions and partner branding
  • a public conference playlist on YouTube
  • a LinkedIn post for each interview

How the library helps a conference

Interviews from past events remain available to browse by conference, topic and speaker, so a conference can be revisited after it has finished. You can see the existing work in the video library.

How to start

Tell us about your event, its dates and what you would like to capture through the contact page. We can then discuss what would suit your conference. Please also tell us which society or organisation is involved, so that any partner branding can be agreed and shown clearly on the videos.

Partnerships and branding are always shown openly on the videos. Read more in our editorial policy.
