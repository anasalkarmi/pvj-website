Reducing low-value care in paediatric ICU

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 1:29 min
  • LT
    Professor Lyvonne TumeProfessor of Critical Care Nursing

Professor Lyvonne Tume, Professor of Critical Care Nursing, discusses a multidisciplinary study identifying low-value practices in paediatric intensive care. She explains how a Delphi process brought together professional perspectives on interventions that may be unnecessary or offer limited benefit. Topics include routine blood sampling, medication review, fasting and the influence of risk aversion on everyday decisions. The interview explores why reducing low-value care matters for children and families as well as staff workload, resources and the environment. Professor Tume encourages teams to examine the evidence behind routine practice and consider where change could improve care.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
