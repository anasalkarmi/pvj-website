Transition from PICU to adult critical care

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 1:21 min
  • NP
    Professor Natalie PattisonProfessor of Clinical Nursing at King's College London

Professor Natalie Pattison, Professor of Clinical Nursing at King's College London, provides insights into the transition from paediatric intensive care to adult critical care. She discusses the preparation adult services need to receive young people, including appropriate resources, staff training and named transition leads. The interview highlights the importance of a structured, shared approach between services while recognising that each person's circumstances and preferences are different. Professor Pattison emphasises involving young people themselves, as well as their families, and understanding what matters to them when planning where and how their care is delivered.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
