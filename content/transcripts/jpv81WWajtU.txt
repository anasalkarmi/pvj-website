KEY POINTS
- The debate on where teenagers go after trauma is about more than the injury, resuscitation and operative treatment.
- Care should cover psychosocial needs, safeguarding, work with the family, and getting back into school and sport.
- An integrated approach should bring expertise to the teenager rather than moving the teenager to adult services alone.
