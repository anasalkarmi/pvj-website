Lessons from the first 100 years of the first university chair of child health in the UK

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 1:39 min
  • Professor Faisal AhmedHolder of the Samson Gemmell Chair of Child Health at the University of Glasgow

Professor Faisal Ahmed, holder of the Samson Gemmell Chair of Child Health at the University of Glasgow, shares his expertise on advancing paediatric care and research, as a collective healthcare community. Reflecting on his latest publication on the lessons from the first 100 years of the first university chair of child health in the UK, this interview highlights how academic leadership and sustained investment in child health research have shaped the evolution of paediatric medicine and improved outcomes for children worldwide This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
