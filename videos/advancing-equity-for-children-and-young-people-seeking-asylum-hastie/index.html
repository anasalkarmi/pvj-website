Advancing Equity for Children and Young People Seeking Asylum and Refuge: Project AEGIS

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 27 August 2025
  • 0:47 min
  • GH
    Grace HastiePolicy Manager for Child Protection and Ethics at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health

Grace Hastie, Policy Manager for Child Protection and Ethics at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, provides insights into Project AEGIS, an exciting National Institute for Health Research (NIHR) funded project hosted at UCL. The focus of the project is supporting children and young people seeking asylum and refuge. Grace highlights the importance of bringing together best practices and existing policy interventions, while also centering the voices and experiences of young people themselves. She discusses the project’s aims to identify what works, address gaps in support, and design new policy interventions to improve outcomes for these vulnerable groups across the country. Grace emphasises the value of collaboration with young people to ensure meaningful and sustainable change. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
