Evaluation of association between snake bite and myocarditis among children

  • RCPCH Conference 2024
  • Birmingham
  • Published 30 December 2024
  • 1:28 min
  • Arun PrasadAll India Institute of Medical Sciences

Arun Prasad, All India Institute of Medical Sciences, discusses his work in evaluating the association between myocarditis and snake envenomation in children. Dr Prasad and his team found a considerably higher incidence of cardiac complications in children after snake envenomation. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2024 in Birmingham, UK.
