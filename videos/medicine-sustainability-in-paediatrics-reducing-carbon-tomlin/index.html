Medicine Sustainability in Paediatrics: Reducing Carbon Footprints in Child Healthcare

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 1:28 min
  • ST
    Steve TomlinDirector of the Children’s Medicines Research & Innovation Centre at Great Ormond Street Hospital

Steve Tomlin, Director of the Children’s Medicines Research & Innovation Centre at Great Ormond Street Hospital, provides insights into the importance of medicine sustainability in paediatrics. The discussion highlights that while much of the NHS carbon footprint from medicines is linked to industry, paediatric medicine has specific considerations that can make a real difference. Tomlin explains that 25% of the NHS carbon footprint is attributable to medicines, and while much of this is outside direct clinician control, there are practical steps that paediatric teams can take to reduce environmental impact. Research shows that liquid medicines have a carbon footprint about 40 times higher than tablets in terms of administration, and oral medicines are far better than intravenous (IV) routes. Tomlin encourages clinicians to transition children from liquids to tablets, and to review IV therapies for opportunities to switch to oral medications, as these changes can significantly reduce the carbon footprint associated with paediatric medicine. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
