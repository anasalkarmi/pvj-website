KEY POINTS
- Point of care ultrasound is a vital tool for clinical decisions, but clinicians must understand their remit and use it judiciously.
- It is a bedside assessment by the treating clinicians, unlike detailed echocardiography done by sonographers or radiologists.
- The learning pathway starts with image acquisition, then interpretation, then applying it to supplement clinical decision making.
