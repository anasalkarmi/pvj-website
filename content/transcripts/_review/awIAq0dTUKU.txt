KEY POINTS
- Of 22 UK centres that replied, 16 had a working model of multidisciplinary care for children with complexity, but there was no consensus on how such a child is defined.
- Most PICUs have no specific time or funding for children with complexity, and 18 of the 22 units had no youth worker allocated.
- Staff reported a high level of stress and burnout associated with the heavy workload of these multidisciplinary meetings and ongoing care.
