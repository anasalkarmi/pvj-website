NIHR Incubator - supporting paediatric critical care researchers

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 2:20 min
  • PR
    Professor Padmanabhan RamnarayanProfessor of Paediatric Critical Care at Imperial College London

Professor Padmanabhan Ramnarayan, Professor of Paediatric Critical Care at Imperial College London, discusses supporting the next generation of paediatric critical-care researchers. He describes the work of the NIHR research incubator, including training, mentorship and help with developing research ideas and grant applications. The interview highlights multidisciplinary participation and the importance of patient and public involvement throughout the research process. Professor Ramnarayan reflects on building a research community, helping colleagues develop their careers and sustaining the collaboration and support created through the incubator.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
