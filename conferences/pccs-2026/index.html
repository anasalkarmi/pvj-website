  • Paediatric Critical Care Society

PVJ joined the Paediatric Critical Care Society community at Lord's in London for PCCS Conference 2026. We spoke with clinicians, nurses, allied health professionals, researchers, students and community partners about the ideas and experiences shaping paediatric critical care.
