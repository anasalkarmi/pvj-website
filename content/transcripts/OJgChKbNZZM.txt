KEY POINTS
- Twenty-five per cent of the NHS carbon footprint is from medicines, and most of that is to do with industry.
- Liquid medicines are probably about 40 times worse than tablets in terms of administration, and oral medicines are far better than IV.
- Children should be moved from liquids onto tablets where possible, and IVs should always be reviewed with a view to moving to oral medicines.
