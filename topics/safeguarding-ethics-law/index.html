Topic

Safeguarding, ethics and law

Child protection, ethical decisions and the legal framework around children's care.

The videos cover how courts approach serious medical-treatment cases involving children, including best interests, and practical preparation for giving evidence in court. They also describe medical photography in child protection examinations, safeguarding in adolescent trauma care and a shared strategy for paediatric and neonatal organ donation.
