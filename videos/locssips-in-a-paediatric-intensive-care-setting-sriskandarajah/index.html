LocSSIPs in a Paediatric Intensive Care Setting

  • RCPCH Conference 2024
  • Birmingham
  • Published 30 December 2024
  • 2:01 min
  • KS
    Kokul SriskandarajahLondon North West Healthcare Trust

Kokul Sriskandarajah, London North West Healthcare Trust, discusses his work in identifying the utilisation of Local Safety Standards for Invasive Procedures (LocSSIP) in the Paediatric Intensive Care Unit of large teaching hospital in London. Following 268 never events in the UK in 2023, LocSSIPs are seen as a fundamental part of ensuring safety when inserting lines in a PICU setting and is paramount for patient safety. The team found significant shortcomings in documentation of LocSSIPs with a clear need to educate medical staff and the better integration into local electronic systems. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2024 in Birmingham, UK.
