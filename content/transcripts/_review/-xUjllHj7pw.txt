KEY POINTS
- An audit of elective spinal cases in Belfast found that only three of 42 patients needed level two or level three interventions.
- Many of these patients could probably have been managed safely on the ward rather than in paediatric intensive care.
- Northern Ireland has no high dependency unit, so work is under way to adapt care environments and prove the need for one.
