- The audit investigated whether the threshold could be lowered from greater than 10 times to greater than five times the upper limit of normal.
- There are more diagnoses via a non-biopsy approach and more symptomatic diagnoses than in the 2005 to 2011 study.
TRANSCRIPT
My project was an audit of coeliac disease in Cardiff and Vale. I was investigating the numbers of paediatric patients diagnosed via biopsy and non-biopsy pathway and comparing this to their IgA-TTG results to investigate whether the threshold for diagnosis could be lowered to greater than five times the upper limit of normal, compared to greater than 10 times the upper limit of normal.

We also compared this to previous results from a study in 2005 to 2011 to compare results. And the main findings were that there are more diagnoses occurring via a non-biopsy approach, and there's also more symptomatic diagnosis occurring.
