Topic

Patient safety and quality improvement

Making care safer and better: standards, early warning systems and improvement projects.

The videos cover escalation and early warning, including Martha's Rule, Ryan's Rule and paediatric early warning systems. Others examine standardised medicine labels, liquid medicine strengths and IV infusions, LocSSIPs for invasive procedures, a lumbar puncture toolkit and low-value care in PICU, along with audits of ventilation weaning and high-flow weaning.
