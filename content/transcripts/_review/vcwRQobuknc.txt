KEY POINTS
- Clinicians going to court are witnesses to fact, and the key advice is generally not to panic because it is not as scary as people think.
- A witness statement is evidence and should be clear, written in language anyone can understand, with medical jargon explained.
- When giving evidence, listen to the question, answer it to the best of your ability without going further, and stay respectful to the family.
