About PVJ

The Paediatric Video Journal (PVJ) is an independent, open-access platform at the forefront of paediatric research dissemination, revolutionising the way knowledge is shared across the paediatric community. By leveraging digital tools, PVJ offers concise, up-to-date information in the form of videos, enabling the rapid and wide-reaching distribution of paediatric research.

As the future of research communication, PVJ provides an innovative approach to sharing the latest findings, making complex paediatric topics accessible to healthcare professionals worldwide. Through this digital medium, we ensure that critical research is not only disseminated efficiently but also engages a global audience, fostering continuous learning and advancement in paediatric care.

By shaping the future of paediatric research, the platform is transforming how information is shared, empowering healthcare professionals to stay informed and improve patient outcomes on a global scale.

What is in the library

The library holds 123 videos, each filed by clinical area and topic, by speaker and by the conference where it was recorded. So far the videos come from three conferences:

  • PCCS Conference 2026, Lord's, London (PCCS is the Paediatric Critical Care Society)
  • RCPCH Conference 2025, Glasgow (RCPCH is the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health)
  • RCPCH Conference 2024, Birmingham

Most videos run for between one and four minutes. Interviewees include clinicians, nurses, allied health professionals, researchers and students, as well as parents and young people who take part in research.

How videos are made

Videos are recorded, edited and then published. Each one appears with the speaker's name and role and a written summary. Videos are hosted on the PVJ YouTube channel and are also shared on the PVJ LinkedIn page.

Who it is for

The content is intended for healthcare professionals and is educational. It is not medical advice, and the views expressed are those of the speakers. Our editorial policy explains how we work.

Where to go next

Start with the clinical areas and topics or the full video library. If you organise a conference or lead a society, see Work with PVJ.
