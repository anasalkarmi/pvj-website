Supporting allied health professionals across PCCS

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 0:58 min
  • Hannah ChildLead Respiratory Physiotherapist in PICU at Birmingham Children’s Hospital and PCCS Council AHP Representative

Key points

  • The role of the AHP representative on PCCS Council is to promote all AHP professions, not only physiotherapy, within the paediatric critical care community.
  • AHPs across the UK are encouraged to attend events, learn, disseminate what they learn, improve practice and share their work.
  • AHPs are encouraged to consider joining PCCS to support collaboration across the UK.

Hannah Child, Lead Respiratory Physiotherapist in PICU at Birmingham Children’s Hospital and PCCS Council AHP Representative, discusses the contribution of allied health professionals to paediatric critical care. She highlights the breadth of professions involved and the value of learning together, sharing practice and connecting through PCCS events. The interview explores opportunities for greater participation and collaboration across the UK. In a concluding message, she encourages allied health professionals to join the Society and help strengthen the network supporting their work within critical-care teams.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
