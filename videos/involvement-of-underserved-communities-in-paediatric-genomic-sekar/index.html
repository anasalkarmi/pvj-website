Involvement of Underserved Communities in Paediatric Genomic Testing

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 22 June 2025
  • 1:00 min
  • SS
    Dr Shivani SekarEast and North Hertfordshire NHS Trust

Dr Shivani Sekar, East and North Hertfordshire NHS Trust, provides insights into the involvement of underserved communities in paediatric genomic testing, as part of an East of England research team. The discussion highlights the barriers faced by these communities at every stage of the patient pathway; from referral and consent to participation in genomic testing and research reporting. Dr Sekar emphasises the importance of reporting demographic characteristics of study populations to better analyse and address these barriers. She encourages leveraging identified solutions to improve participation and ensure that genomic testing and research are accessible and inclusive for all children and families. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
