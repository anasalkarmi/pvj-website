KEY POINTS
- A three-round Delphi study across UK staff from many disciplines identified the top 10 low-value care practices in paediatric ICUs.
- The top five included unnecessary blood gases and bloods, overuse of medicines such as PPIs, and unnecessary or prolonged fasting around procedures and extubation.
- Risk aversion affects care, the time it takes to deliver, and cost and environmental impact, and the paper is published open access in Nursing in Critical Care.

We wanted to really identify what are the top 10, say, low value care practices that we do in paediatric ICU. So this was a three-round Delphi and we ended up coming up with 10.
