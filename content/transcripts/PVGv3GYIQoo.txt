KEY POINTS
- Project AEGIS is an NIHR-funded project hosted at UCL about children and young people seeking asylum and refuge.
- The aim is to bring together learning from existing interventions and policy pieces and to talk to the young people themselves.
- The project will design policy interventions and look at what works and what the gaps are.
TRANSCRIPT
So my name is Grace Hastie. I am a policy manager at the college and I'm an investigator on Project AEGIS. This is a really exciting NIHR funded project. It's hosted at UCL and it's all about children and young people seeking asylum and refuge.

We know that there are so many good interventions and policy pieces that aim to support these young people. But we want to bring that learning together, and crucially we want to talk to the young people themselves, and that's exactly what this NIHR grant is all about. So we're going to design policy interventions. We're going to look at what works, what the gaps are, and hopefully roll out some really exciting work for these young people across the country.
