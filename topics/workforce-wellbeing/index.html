Topic

Workforce and wellbeing

Careers, staffing, teamwork and the wellbeing of people who care for children.

The videos cover staff wellbeing after a major incident, nursing careers in PICU, the value of critical-care nursing and the contribution of allied health professionals. They also describe educator roles, rapid review clinics, hospital at home, trainee involvement in research and Registrar Ready days for the tier 2 rota.
