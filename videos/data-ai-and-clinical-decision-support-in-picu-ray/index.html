Data, AI and clinical decision support in PICU

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 2:53 min
  • SR
    Dr Samiran RayConsultant in Paediatric Intensive Care at Great Ormond Street Hospital

Dr Samiran Ray, Consultant in Paediatric Intensive Care at Great Ormond Street Hospital, provides insights into the role of data and artificial intelligence in paediatric critical care. He describes the volume of information generated by bedside monitors, electronic health records and clinical interactions, and the challenge of bringing it together to support decisions. The interview explores the development of data repositories through collaboration between clinical, engineering and mathematical teams. Dr Ray considers how future decision-support tools could help clinicians assess uncertainty and understand patterns in critical illness, supporting rather than replacing clinical judgement.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
