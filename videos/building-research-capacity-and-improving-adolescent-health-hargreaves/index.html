Building Research Capacity and Improving Adolescent Health Through a Multisectoral Approach

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 2:10 min
  • DH
    Professor Dougal HargreavesImperial College London

Professor Dougal Hargreaves, Imperial College London, discusses a project focused on building research capacity in under-researched areas of paediatrics and child health. He highlights a striking disparity: children and young people make up about 20% of the population and use 10% of NHS resources, yet receive only 5% of research funding. The project aims to increase investment in child health research to improve health outcomes and lifelong wellbeing. Professor Hargreaves particularly emphasises adolescent health, advocating for a more multisectoral and joined-up approach. He notes that young people do not see their physical health, mental health, educational experience, and social care as separate issues, but rather as interconnected parts of their lives. The current system is often fragmented, with young people receiving support from multiple services that are not well-coordinated. The project seeks to join up data, literature, and narratives to demonstrate the benefits of integrated investment in health and education, ultimately leading to better, more efficient, and cost-effective outcomes for young people. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
