KEY POINTS
- Children in low and middle income countries often miss vaccines that children in high income countries get, including measles and rubella.
- A micro needle patch is pain-free, needs no cold chain and can be given by anybody after minimal training.
- New vaccines should target pathogens that burden low and middle income countries, such as group A streptococcus and viral haemorrhagic fevers.
