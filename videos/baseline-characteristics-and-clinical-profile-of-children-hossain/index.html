Baseline characteristics and clinical profile of children presenting with TB disease

  • RCPCH Conference 2024
  • Birmingham
  • Published 30 December 2024
  • 1:49 min
  • AH
    Aniqah HossainRoyal London Hospital

Aniqah Hossain, Royal London Hospital, discusses her work in describing the characteristics and clinical profile of children presenting with TB disease to a large NHS Trust in East London. In the predominantly south Asian cohort, female predominance was seen. Pulmonary TB was the most common clinical phenotype found. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2024 in Birmingham, UK.
