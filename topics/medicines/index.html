Medicines and prescribing

Safe, effective and sustainable use of medicines for children.

The videos cover standardising medicine labels, liquid medicine strengths and IV infusion concentrations, with smart pumps and ready-made syringes. Other subjects include burosumab, buccal oxycodone in paediatric palliative care, hydroxycarbamide, biologic therapies, the carbon footprint of medicines and animated medicine information for children.
