Key points

  • Seeing thousands of paediatricians in one room under the same roof is exciting for a paediatrician from Australia.
  • The conference in Australia is for physicians and paediatricians together.

Professor Nitin Kapur, President of the Paediatric & Child Health Division of the Royal Australasian College of Physicians, shares his enthusiasm for the large gathering of paediatricians at the RCPCH conference. He expresses his excitement at seeing thousands of paediatricians in one room, a rare experience for him as an attendee from Australia, and wishes the RCPCH the very best for the future. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.

Read the transcript

Automatic transcript, lightly edited for readability. It may contain errors, so check it against the video before quoting.

I am actually so excited and amazed to see so many paediatricians in one conference. Our conference in Australia is for physicians and paediatricians, but here to see thousands of paediatricians in one room under the same roof actually is quite exciting for me as a paediatrician coming from Australia. And I wish RCPCH the very best of luck for the future.
