KEY POINTS
- Serious medical treatment cases involving children come to court under principles centred on the best interests of the child in the widest sense.
- There is a presumption that steps will be taken to preserve life, and the court considers the views of the parents, family and clinicians.
- A working group is aiming to produce clear written guidance to help clinicians, families and lawyers in these difficult and sensitive cases.

So I'm a High Court judge in the Family Division and I deal with cases of serious medical treatment of children.
