Supporting patient safety in paediatrics: RCPCH resources and initiatives

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 27 August 2025
  • 1:30 min
  • Jonathan BamberHead of Quality Improvement at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health

Key points

  • The RCPCH has been developing programmes such as SAFE and web pages and resources around patient safety.
  • The patient safety portal includes podcasts on safety culture, psychological safety, learning from harm and inequality and safety.
  • A safety e-bulletin goes to RCPCH members every two months, and non-members can see previous editions on the website.

Jonathan Bamber, Head of Quality Improvement at the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, provides insights into the ongoing efforts to support patient safety for children, young people, and RCPCH members. The discussion highlights key initiatives such as the SAFE programme and the development of web pages and resources dedicated to patient safety. Mr Bamber introduces the RCPCH patient safety portal, which offers a wealth of information including podcasts on safety culture, psychological safety, learning from harm, and addressing inequality in safety. He also details the launch of a safety e-bulletin, distributed to members every two months, summarizing the latest developments in paediatric patient safety. Mr Bamber encourages all members to engage with these resources and invites non-members to access previous editions via the RCPCH website. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.

Hello, my name is Jonathan Bamber. I'm the head of quality improvement at the RCPCH, and today I'd like to talk about patient safety and how we can support all of our members and children and young people in this area.

We have been working for the last few years on programmes like the SAFE programme and production of web pages and resources around patient safety. We have a patient safety portal which collects information around patient safety, where there will be a link on this video for you to look at. Within that includes things like podcasts on safety, safety culture, psychological safety, how to learn from harm, inequality and safety, and other aspects to support you in patient safety.

To help you with patient safety, we've also launched a safety e-bulletin. If you are an RCPCH member, you will be receiving this every two months. There was one that went out in December of 24, another one in February, and it will happen every two months from then. This is a summary or a snapshot of what's happening in paediatric patient safety for you to be able to get quick links onto that information. If you're not a member, you can go to our website, that same portal, and you can see previous editions. Thank you and goodbye.
