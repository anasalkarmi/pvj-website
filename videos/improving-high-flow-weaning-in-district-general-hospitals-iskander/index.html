Improving high-flow weaning in district general hospitals

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 3:03 min
  • DI
    Diana IskanderPaediatric Consultant at William Harvey Hospital

Diana Iskander, Paediatric Consultant at William Harvey Hospital, discusses a retrospective audit of high-flow weaning in two district general hospitals. She describes reviewing adherence to a regional guideline and examining why practice did not always follow its oxygen-based weaning approach. The interview explores the importance of considering respiratory distress and work of breathing alongside oxygen requirements. Diana reflects on the limitations of the audit, steps taken to improve clinicians’ awareness and changes to local guidance, as well as sharing the findings to inform future guideline development.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
