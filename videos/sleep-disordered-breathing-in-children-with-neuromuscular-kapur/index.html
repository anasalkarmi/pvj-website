Sleep Disordered Breathing in Children With Neuromuscular Disorders

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 0:42 min
  • NK
    Professor Nitin KapurPresident, Paediatric & Child Health Division, Royal Australasian College of Physicians

Professor Nitin Kapur; President, Paediatric & Child Health Division, Royal Australasian College of Physicians, highlights the critical importance of maintaining a low threshold for the early detection of sleep-disordered breathing (SDB) in children with neuromuscular disorders. Early recognition and intervention can prevent significant morbidity and mortality, improve quality of life, and support better long-term outcomes for affected children and their families. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
