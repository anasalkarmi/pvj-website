Metabolic Dysfunction Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD) in children: use of fibroscan

  • RCPCH Conference 2024
  • Birmingham
  • Published 30 December 2024
  • 1:27 min
  • CM
    Caitlin MurphyKing's College Hospital

Caitlin Murphy, King's College Hospital, discusses her work in identifying identifying fibroscan markers of liver stiffness and steatosis, with anthriopometry, ultrasound and biochemical liver functions tests in 76 children with MASLD. The majority of children were found to have evidence of significant steatosis and mild fibrosis, both of which are completely reversible at the stage of identification. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2024 in Birmingham, UK.
