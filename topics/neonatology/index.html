Topic

Neonatology

Care of newborn and preterm infants, in hospital and at home.

The videos cover bedside laryngeal ultrasound in infants, a pilot of oral antibiotics at home for newborns with suspected early-onset infection, and point-of-care ultrasound. They also describe neonatal brain MRI over 15 years and Neotree, which supports newborn care in low-resource settings.
