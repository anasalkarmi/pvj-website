Topic

Children, young people and families

Lived experience, co-production and the voices of children, young people and parents.

The videos cover patient and public involvement in critical-care research, including the lived experience of a parent and a former PICU patient, and co-production with young people. Others describe Project AEGIS for children seeking asylum, CoLab for children with medical complexity, Medicines for Children information, Ryan's Rule and why some families choose not to vaccinate.
