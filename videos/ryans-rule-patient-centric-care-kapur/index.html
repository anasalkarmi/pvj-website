Ryan's Rule: patient centric care

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 0:45 min
  • Professor Nitin KapurPresident, Paediatric & Child Health Division, Royal Australasian College of Physicians

Key points

  • Patients and parents should be part of the healthcare journey and the decision making for children and adults.
  • Ryan's Rule encompasses the principles of patient centred care, including listening and coordinated care.
  • Experience of Ryan's Rule in Queensland can help the NHS incorporate these principles for the success of Martha's Rule.

Professor Nitin Kapur; President, Paediatric & Child Health Division, Royal Australasian College of Physicians, discusses Ryan’s Rule and its significance in promoting patient-centric care within the healthcare system. Professor Kapur emphasises the importance of listening to patients and their families and integrating their perspectives into clinical decision-making. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.

Read the transcript

Automatic transcript, lightly edited for readability. It may contain errors, so check it against the video before quoting.

The patient centric care is the future of medicine. We need to make parents, we need to make patients part of all healthcare journey for children and adults. They need to be part of the decision making. And Ryan's Rule actually encompasses all principles of patient centered care. This includes listening to the parent or the patient, making them part of their healthcare decision-making as well. And coordinated care.

I bring with myself 10 to 14 years of experience of Ryan's Rule in Queensland and I want to help the NHS to incorporate those principles for success of Martha's Rule. That's my take-home message.
