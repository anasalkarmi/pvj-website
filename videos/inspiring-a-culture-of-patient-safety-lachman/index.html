Inspiring a Culture of Patient Safety

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 1:51 min
  • PL
    Dr Peter LachmanLead Faculty for Quality Improvement at the RCPI (Royal College of Physicians of Ireland)

Dr Peter Lachman, Lead Faculty for Quality Improvement at the RCPI (Royal College of Physicians of Ireland), shares his expert perspective on embedding a culture of safety within medical education and clinical practice. Drawing on decades of experience in patient safety, he emphasises the importance of making patient safety a core element of medical training, rather than a reactive concept introduced after graduation. Dr Lachman advocates for proactive, habitual safety practices in hospitals, and calls for a significant transformation in the design of medical education and healthcare systems to ensure safety is prioritised every day. This approach has the potential to improve outcomes for both patients and staff and to raise standards of care on a global scale. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
