KEY POINTS
- Data that looks at wide, broad populations of children helps show whether problems are occurring across the entire population of paediatric critical care.
- Population data can then help identify PICUs, children or conditions to focus on, narrowing down and focusing research.
- Real-world data sources like PICANet can help spot problems before we even realise they are there.
