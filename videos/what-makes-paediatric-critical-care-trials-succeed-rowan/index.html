What makes paediatric critical care trials succeed?

  • PCCS Conference 2026
  • Lord's, London
  • Published 7 November 2026
  • 2:02 min
  • KR
    Professor Kathy RowanFounder and Scientific Advisor at ICNARC

Professor Kathy Rowan, Founder and Scientific Advisor at ICNARC, provides insights into the achievements of the UK paediatric critical care community in delivering randomised clinical trials. She reflects on the collaborative culture behind this work and the contribution of expertise in methodology, data and analysis. The interview highlights the importance of high-quality trial delivery, including complete data collection and adherence to the interventions being evaluated. Professor Rowan explains how these shared efforts have helped produce a substantial evidence base and enabled findings from paediatric critical care research to reach an international audience through major scientific publications.

This interview took place at the PCCS Annual Scientific Meeting 2026 in London.
