Local Extubation in Children with Status Epilepticus

  • RCPCH Conference 2025
  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025
  • 1:17 min
  • KS
    Dr Kokul SriskandarajahChelsea and Westminster Hospital

Dr Kokul Sriskandarajah, Chelsea and Westminster Hospital, provides insights into a project focused on improving the management of children with status epilepticus at a busy district general hospital in London. The discussion highlights a study conducted both prospectively and retrospectively, following the introduction of new guidelines for local extubation in children who require intubation due to prolonged seizures. Dr Sriskandarajah describes how, prior to guideline implementation, nine children could not be extubated locally due to a lack of clinician knowledge and awareness of the guidelines. After targeted teaching and discussion of the Children’s Acute Transport Service guidelines, the team was able to safely extubate two children locally, thereby avoiding unnecessary transfers to paediatric intensive care and conserving valuable PICU beds in London. He emphasizes that this approach can be adopted by other trusts, supporting safe, local extubation for children with status epilepticus and improving resource utilization. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
