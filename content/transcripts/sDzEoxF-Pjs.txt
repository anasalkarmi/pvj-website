KEY POINTS
- Medicines for Children was founded in 2006 and turns complex medicine information into guidance that families can read easily and use at home.
- The website has over 220 children's medicines and about 2 million downloads a year, but many families still do not know about it.
- Clinicians are invited to help review information every 3 years, and a parent-held app for children with complex needs is available on Apple devices.
