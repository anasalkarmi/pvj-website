KEY POINTS
- A new strategy for paediatric and neonatal organ donation was launched, and the relationship between NHS Blood and Transplant and PCCS is described as integral.
- The aim is to ensure organ donation is offered as an end of life care option.
- The aim is also to ensure that children who require an organ can find a suitable transplant.
TRANSCRIPT
So the relationship between NHS Blood and Transplant and PCCS is integral. We are here to launch our latest strategy in terms of paediatric and neonatal organ donation and how we can work better with the paediatric care society to ensure that organ donation is offered as an end of life care option, but also ensuring that the children that we care for who require an organ, they find a suitable or possible transplant.

So these are life-saving sort of potentially interventions that we can make a difference, and it's only because of the close relationship between NHSBT and PCCS that this is possible.
