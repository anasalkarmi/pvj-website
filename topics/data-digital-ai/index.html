Topic

Data, digital and AI

Data science, digital tools and artificial intelligence in child health.

The videos cover PICU data repositories and decision-support tools, population-level data such as PICANet, and AI tools that analyse video, audio and questionnaires to detect ADHD and depression. They also describe virtual reality simulation for autism spectrum disorder, animated medicine information with QR codes on pharmacy labels and the real-time data used by Neotree.
