KEY POINTS
- Dermatology is not a 24-hour service but A&E is, and A&E clinicians did not feel confident managing children's skin presentations out of hours.
- A guideline was created for core presentations, with management plans, escalation of treatment and images showing a range of skin tones.
- Clinician confidence in managing dermatology presentations improved by more than 70% after the guideline was implemented.
TRANSCRIPT
So whilst working in the [unclear] busy children's A&E department, we realised that there was a big gap with how clinicians were feeling in their confidence and management of dermatology presentations to A&E. So whilst dermatology isn't a 24-hour service, A&E is. So we noticed that children were coming in with either a severe deterioration of an existing or a new dermatological condition and A&E clinicians just weren't feeling confident with managing that by themselves out of hours.

So what we sought to do was create a guideline to be used in the A&E specifically for paediatric dermatology presentations. So we started out by doing a survey of what conditions came to the A&E often and where the knowledge gap was. We then sought advice from our dermatology colleagues and we presented this at the [unclear] and we created a guideline of core presentations that come to A&E as well as the management plan and how to escalate treatment out of hours so that by the time you've sent the child home you have given them some management.

So we also made sure that we included images that had children with all different skin tones and backgrounds which represented our diverse population. And then we resurveyed clinician confidence after implementing the guideline and we improved clinician confidence and management of dermatology presentations by more than 70%.

So really the purpose of this project was to show that if you can identify knowledge gaps in where clinicians feel that they need that help, you produce a resource for them and it gives clinicians more confidence. It gives better outcomes for patients and we can learn and keep looking for those knowledge gaps in our management.
