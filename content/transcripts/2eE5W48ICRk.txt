KEY POINTS
- A retrospective study looked at unplanned readmission to PICU within 48 hours of discharge over two years, from April 2023 to April 2025.
- No clear most common risk factor emerged, so the team concentrated on modifiable ones such as respiratory rate, oxygen requirement and heart rate.
- The team plans a risk assessment tool before discharge and ongoing follow-up with the wider team after discharge.
