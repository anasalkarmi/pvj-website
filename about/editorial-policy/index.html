Editorial policy

This policy explains what The Paediatric Video Journal (PVJ) publishes, how videos come about, and what you can expect from us if you appear in a video or watch one.

Purpose and audience

PVJ publishes short videos so that the people who work in and around child health can share and learn from each other's work. The content is intended for healthcare professionals and is educational.

How videos come about

So far, our videos are interviews recorded on site at conferences with presenters and delegates. We work with the organising society for each event. Speakers agree to be recorded and for the interview to be published. Each video is published with the speaker's name and role and a written summary.

Speakers' views and medical advice

Views expressed in the videos are those of the speakers. They are not the views of PVJ, and they do not represent the position of any conference or society unless the speaker says so. Nothing on this site is medical advice. It should not replace the judgement of a qualified clinician or local guidance.

Accuracy and corrections

We aim to publish summaries and captions that accurately reflect what was said. If you think something is wrong, please tell us through the contact page. We will review the error and correct it where needed.

Consent and removal requests

If you have appeared in a PVJ video and would like it reviewed or removed, use the contact page and tell us which video you mean.

Partnerships and branding

Where a society or organisation has worked with us on a conference, its branding is shown openly on the videos.

Copyright and reuse

This work is protected by copyright laws and treaties around the world. All rights are reserved. You are welcome to link to our videos or embed them through YouTube. Please do not re-upload or edit them without permission. See the terms of use for more detail, or ask through the contact page.
