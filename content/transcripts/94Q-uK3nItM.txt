KEY POINTS
- A single-centre review covered 82 children with severe traumatic brain injury admitted to paediatric intensive care between 2016 and 2025.
- Only 30 of the 66 ventilated patients were monitored with an ICP bolt, and ventilated patients can be monitored safely without one.
- The review was single-centre and retrospective, so further multicentre analysis is needed on which patients should get ICP monitoring.
