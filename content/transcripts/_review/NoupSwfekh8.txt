- Traumatic brain injury is a severe cause of paediatric neurodisability and intensive care admissions, and ICP monitoring is the standard recommendation.
- The evidence behind the Brain Trauma Foundation guidelines is quite variable and often comes from adult cases.
- A retrospective study at the Royal London Hospital used 2016 to 2025 data to see whether children with traumatic brain injury received ICP monitoring.
