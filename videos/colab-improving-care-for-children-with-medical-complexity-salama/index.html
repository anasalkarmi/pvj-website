CoLab: improving care for children with medical complexity through partnership and innovation

  • Glasgow
  • Published 23 June 2025

  • Dr Mary SalamaConsultant paediatrician at Birmingham Children’s Hospital

Key points

  • CoLab is a free partnership of people looking at how to improve care for children with medical complexity.
  • Children with medical complexity are a vulnerable group and high healthcare users in both community and acute settings.
  • Organisations are invited to an open mic session, and a dragon's den with experienced parent carers is hoped for.

https://www.colabpartnership.org.uk/

Dr Mary Salama, consultant paediatrician at Birmingham Children’s Hospital, introduces CoLab—a partnership initiative dedicated to improving care for children with medical complexity. These children represent a vulnerable group and are high users of healthcare services in both community and acute settings. CoLab aims to connect professionals, carers, and families to share ideas, learn from each other, and collaboratively develop new approaches to care. Dr Salama highlights the free, open nature of the initiative and invites all interested parties to participate in open mic sessions and upcoming events, including a “dragon’s den” where experienced parent carers will provide feedback on new ideas. She encourages attendees to join the initiative, share their insights, and help drive meaningful improvements for children with complex medical needs. This interview took place at the RCPCH conference 2025 in Glasgow, Scotland.
