Topic

Adolescent health

Young people's health and the move from children's to adult services.

The videos cover adult critical care services that receive young people after PICU, transition to adult services for young people with complex conditions, and developmentally appropriate healthcare across a regional network. Others describe adolescent major trauma and child health research investment across health, education and social care.
