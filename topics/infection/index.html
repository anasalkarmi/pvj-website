Topic

Infection

Infectious disease in childhood, including sepsis, vaccination and the use of antibiotics.

The videos cover the Phoenix criteria for sepsis and septic shock, oral antibiotics for newborns with suspected early-onset infection and point-of-care testing for Group A Streptococcus. They also describe TB disease in children, global vaccination in low- and middle-income countries and why some families choose not to vaccinate.
