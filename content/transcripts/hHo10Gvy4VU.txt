KEY POINTS
- A retrospective audit in a paediatric ICU found a median eight-hourly delay in starting weaning and a median three-hourly delay in sustaining it.
- Spontaneous breathing trials and four-hourly comfort scores were not performed consistently.
- Next steps include a checklist, guideline or protocol, and an electronic-record dashboard showing whether a patient can safely be weaned.
